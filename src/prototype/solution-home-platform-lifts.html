SOLUTION DETAIL

Home & Platform Lifts

Compact, design-led lifts for private residences, duplexes, villas, and low-rise applications. Minimal construction footprint, quiet operation, and seamless integration with residential interiors.

WHAT IS IT?

A lift designed for the home, not adapted from a commercial product.

Home and platform lifts are compact vertical-transport systems engineered specifically for residential settings. They require minimal shaft space, operate quietly, and are designed to integrate visually with private interiors — from a modern duplex to a traditional villa.

Platform lifts offer a particularly flexible solution for accessibility and low-rise applications, with minimal construction requirements and the ability to serve existing buildings where a full lift shaft is not practical.

WHO IS IT FOR?

Homeowners who want mobility without compromising design.

    Luxury homeowners adding vertical mobility to a duplex or villa

  • —

    Families planning for long-term accessibility and ageing in place

  • —

MAIN ADVANTAGES

Why choose a home or platform lift?

Compact footprint

Minimal shaft and machine-room requirements — fits into spaces where a conventional lift cannot.

Quiet operation

Designed for residential noise levels — no disruptive mechanical sounds in living spaces.

Design integration

Cabin finishes, doors, and controls selected to match the interior architecture of the home.

Energy efficient

Low power consumption suitable for residential electrical supply — no heavy commercial load.

Safety features

Modern safety systems including emergency lowering, battery backup, and door sensors.

Minimal construction

Platform lifts in particular can be installed with minimal building work — ideal for retrofits.
