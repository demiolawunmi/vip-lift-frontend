MODERNISATION & UPGRADES

Extend the life of your existing lift — without full replacement.

Modernisation upgrades controllers, drives, cabins, doors, and safety components to current standards. It's often the most cost-effective way to improve reliability, safety, and performance without removing and replacing the entire lift system.

WHEN TO MODERNISE

When the lift still has structural life — but the technology has moved on.

Many older lift systems have mechanically sound shafts, rails, and structures — but outdated controllers, inefficient drives, worn cabins, or obsolete safety components.

Modernisation targets the components that matter most for safety, reliability, and user experience — extending the useful life of the installation at a fraction of full replacement cost.

WHAT CAN BE UPGRADED

Modernisation components.

Control System

Controller Upgrade

Replace obsolete relay-based or early-generation controllers with modern microprocessor systems — improved reliability, diagnostics, and performance.

Drive System

Drive Modernisation

Upgrade to variable-voltage variable-frequency (VVVF) drives for smoother ride quality, reduced energy consumption, and less mechanical wear.

Cabin

Cabin Refurbishment

New cabin finishes, lighting, panels, flooring, and handrails — transforming the passenger experience without changing the mechanical system.

Doors

Door Operator Upgrade

Modern door operators with improved sensors, smoother operation, and better safety — reducing door-related faults and improving passenger safety.

Safety

Safety System Update

Upgrade safety components — overspeed governors, buffers, emergency phones, light curtains — to meet current standards and regulations.

Fixtures

Button & Indicator Upgrade

Modern call buttons, car operating panels, and floor indicators — improving usability, accessibility, and the overall passenger experience.

THE MODERNISATION PROCESS

Assess. Recommend. Execute.

  • Condition assessment

    Full inspection of the existing lift system — identifying which components need upgrading and which have remaining service life.

  • Modernisation recommendation

    A phased upgrade plan prioritising safety, reliability, and compliance — with clear scope, timeline, and budget.

  • Component specification

    Selecting compatible, current-generation components that work with the existing structure and mechanical systems.

  • Installation & commissioning

    Carrying out the upgrade with minimal disruption to building operation — including testing, certification, and handover.

  • Ongoing maintenance

    The modernised system enters our maintenance programme — ensuring the upgraded components perform reliably for years to come.
