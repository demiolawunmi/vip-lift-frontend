MAINTENANCE & SERVICING

The relationship begins at handover, not ends.

VIP Lift Nigeria provides preventive maintenance, routine servicing, fault diagnosis, repairs, replacement components, and maintenance planning — for lifts we installed and for existing systems under our care.

WHY MAINTENANCE MATTERS

A lift is a long-term commitment — not a one-time purchase.

Regular maintenance keeps lifts safe, reliable, and compliant. Without it, even the best equipment degrades — leading to breakdowns, safety risks, and costly emergency repairs.

We offer structured maintenance programmes tailored to the building type, usage level, and equipment age. Whether we installed the lift or inherited an existing system, our technical team provides the ongoing care it needs.

MAINTENANCE SERVICES

What our maintenance programme covers.

Preventive Servicing

Scheduled maintenance visits — lubrication, adjustment, cleaning, and inspection of mechanical and electrical components to prevent failures before they occur.

Fault Diagnosis

Technical assessment of lift faults — identifying root causes, not just symptoms, and recommending the correct repair approach.

Emergency Repairs

Rapid response to lift breakdowns — getting the system back in safe operation as quickly as possible, with proper diagnosis and repair.

Replacement Components

Genuine and compatible spare parts supply — ropes, controllers, buttons, door operators, and other critical components.

Safety Compliance Checks

Regular safety inspections to ensure the lift meets current standards — including door sensors, emergency systems, and overload protection.

Maintenance Planning

Long-term maintenance schedules for building portfolios — forecasting service visits, component replacements, and modernisation timing.

EXISTING LIFT SYSTEMS

We maintain lifts we didn't install.

If your building has an existing lift system — whether installed by another company or inherited with the property — we can assess its condition, recommend a maintenance approach, and bring it under our service programme.

An initial condition assessment identifies current safety issues, wear levels, and any immediate repair needs. From there, we develop a maintenance plan that fits the equipment, the building, and the budget.
