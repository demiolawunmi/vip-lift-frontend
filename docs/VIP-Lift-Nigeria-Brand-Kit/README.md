# VIP Lift Nigeria Brand Kit

This is the completed brand system for VIP Lift Nigeria, a Nigerian lift-solutions partner focused on supply, installation, repair, servicing and maintenance for homes, businesses and public buildings.

## Brand direction

Premium Nigerian, modern architectural and engineering-led. The system uses dark navy for depth, silver and white for clarity, and emerald as a restrained accent.

## Core slogan

**VALUE. INNOVATION. PRESTIGE.**

## Folder guide

- `01-Logo` contains the supplied official logo artwork in PNG, SVG and PDF formats. These files are the only approved brand marks.
- `02-Colours` contains the five-page colour guide and its editable PowerPoint master.
- `03-Typography` contains the six-page typography guide, editable master and licensed Google Fonts files.
- `04-Brand-Patterns` contains three identity-derived pattern systems plus architectural background imagery.
- `05-Templates` contains editable and production-ready business cards, letterhead, quotation, company profile, email signature, social assets, cabin plate and the guidelines master.
- `06-Exported-Assets` consolidates production PDF, PNG, JPG and SVG files.
- `VIP-Lift-Nigeria-Brand-Guidelines.pdf` is the main source of truth for brand application.

## Logo use

Use light artwork on dark navy or emerald backgrounds and navy artwork on white or pale backgrounds. Do not stretch, rotate, recolour, outline, shadow or recreate the wordmark. Use a no-slogan lockup when the slogan would reproduce too small.

## Colour use

Midnight Navy and white carry most layouts. Silver provides premium neutrality. Emerald is reserved for calls to action, selected rules and concise highlights. CMYK values are starting approximations; approve physical proofs before production.

## Typography

- Cormorant Garamond: premium display headings.
- Inter: body copy, specifications, quotations and interfaces.
- Montserrat: short uppercase labels and technical details.
- The VIP LIFT wordmark is custom artwork, not a font.

The included font files are distributed under the SIL Open Font License. Install them before editing masters for the closest visual match. Recommended fallbacks are Baskerville, Avenir Next and Arial.

## Patterns

Use the symbol monogram for covers and dividers, diagonal elevation for campaign graphics, and architectural shaft lines for technical documents. Keep opacity controlled so patterns never compete with copy.

## Templates and sample details

Editable examples use the reserved `.example` domain and fictional personnel. Replace names, phone numbers, addresses, technical values, prices, warranty terms and commercial conditions before publication or contractual use.

## Export formats

- SVG: scalable logos and vector patterns.
- PDF: print and client-facing documents.
- PNG: transparent logos and sharp digital assets.
- JPG: photographic or flattened previews where transparency is unnecessary.
- PPTX and DOCX: editable Office-ready source files.
- HTML: compact email-signature implementation.

Unfinished scaffolding, empty source markers, duplicate experiments and temporary render files have been removed from this delivery.
